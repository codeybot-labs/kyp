Four rules every launch follows

The terms are public before anyone buys, and the lock is built into the launch itself.

1

Everyone's visible

Size, terms and milestones are public. Wallets are revealed in waves after launch, smallest first.

2

Everyone's locked

Presale tokens enter the lock in the same transaction that opens the pool.

3

One price

All fills settle together at the opening price. Nobody gets a cheaper entry.

4

Unlock at milestones

TWAP-verified FDV targets, such as $10m. The community roots for presalers to get there.
